The origin

A recipe older than the shop

Elena Papadópoulos left Athens in 1923 with a copper briki, a sack of green beans, and her grandmother's method for a coffee thick enough to read fortunes in. She opened a single counter, ground everything by hand, and refused to rush a single cup.

A century on, we still roast in small batches and still won't hurry the pour. Every cup carries the same slow ceremony she brought across the sea — sand-brewed, twice-strained, and finished the way it always was.
